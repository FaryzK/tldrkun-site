Weirdly

The weird logic of everyday life.

Weirdly is a YouTube channel of short, funny explanations of the strange things people do every day, the reasons hiding behind them, and the rules nobody agreed to.

The Weirdly publishing app

The channel's owner uses a small private app to publish the channel's own videos to YouTube: it uploads each finished video and sets its title, description, thumbnail and release time. It signs in to YouTube only with the channel owner's permission, acts only on that one channel, and is not offered to anyone else.

How it handles data is set out in the privacy policy.
